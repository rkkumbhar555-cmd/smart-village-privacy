Smart Village

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Smart Village is an independent citizen-services application designed to help villagers conveniently discover government services, village announcements, weather information and other useful village resources, including local business listings, news links and agricultural market-price information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you use the Smart Village application.

By using Smart Village, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Independent Application

Smart Village is independently developed and operated. It is not an official government application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected with the Government of India, Government of Maharashtra, UIDAI, Income Tax Department, any Gram Panchayat, municipality, or other government department or authority unless expressly stated otherwise.

3. Information We May Collect

Smart Village is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

Depending on how the application is used, the following information may be processed:

  • Administrator phone number used for administrator authentication.
  • Firebase Authentication information associated with an administrator account.
  • Administrator authentication identifiers used to verify authorized access.
  • Village selection information such as state, district, taluka and village.
  • Information voluntarily entered by an administrator when creating village announcements.
  • Business listing information that may be displayed in the application, such as business name, category, description, contact number, WhatsApp details, address, photographs or banner images, and location details.
  • Information processed by external providers when the app retrieves or displays weather, news or agricultural market-price information. The information processed depends on the relevant provider and service.

Smart Village does not require villagers to create an account or provide a phone number merely to browse the public village information and government-service links.

4. Administrator Authentication

Smart Village uses Firebase Authentication with phone verification for authorized administrators.

Phone numbers and authentication information may be processed by Firebase to provide the authentication service.

Administrator access is restricted through authentication and application authorization controls.

Villagers do not use the administrator authentication system.

5. Village Announcements

Authorized administrators may create, update or delete village announcements.

Announcement information may be stored using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Administrators should not enter unnecessary personal, confidential or sensitive information into public village announcements.

6. Weather Information

Smart Village may request weather information from an external weather service using the selected village's geographic coordinates.

Weather information is used to display general weather conditions, forecasts and farmer-oriented weather alerts.

Weather information is provided for general informational purposes and should not be treated as an official emergency warning.

7. External Government Websites

Smart Village provides links to external websites operated by government departments or other service providers.

When you select an external service, the application may open the respective website in your device's browser.

Important: Smart Village does not operate, control, process, approve, reject or guarantee applications or services provided by external websites.

Information submitted on an external website is governed by the privacy policy and terms of that website.

Users should verify the website address and should not share passwords, OTPs or confidential information with unauthorized persons.

8. Third-Party Services

Smart Village uses certain third-party services to provide its functionality.

  • Google Firebase Authentication for administrator authentication.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore for authorized administrator and announcement data, and for storing agricultural market-price records displayed in the app.
  • An external weather service for weather and forecast information.
  • The Government of India's Open Government Data Platform (data.gov.in), using AGMARKNET agricultural market data, as the source for market-price records. The app's synchronization process retrieves this data and stores records in Firebase Cloud Firestore for the app to display.
  • External news websites or providers for news content or links.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Smart Village does not control the privacy practices, availability or content of external services.

9. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Smart Village may be used to:

  • Authenticate authorized administrators.
  • Protect administrator-only functionality.
  • Manage village announcements.
  • Display local business listings and promotions.
  • Display news headlines, links or other news information.
  • Retrieve, store and display agricultural market-price information.
  • Display weather information and alerts.
  • Provide the application's core functionality.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.

10. Information Sharing

Smart Village does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate application functionality, such as Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Firestore, the weather service, the Government of India's Open Government Data Platform for AGMARKNET market-price data, and external news or service providers.

Business listing information and public village announcements may be displayed to app users.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process or a valid governmental or regulatory request.

11. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information processed by the application.

Firestore access controls and Firebase authentication are used to restrict administrator functionality.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant application functionality, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.

Public village announcements may remain available until they are deleted, deactivated or expire according to application functionality.

13. Account Deletion and Data Deletion

Smart Village currently provides administrator authentication only for authorized administrators.

An administrator who wants their authentication account and associated application data reviewed for deletion may contact the developer using the developer contact information published with the Smart Village application.

Deletion requests may be subject to verification to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

Some information may be retained where retention is required by law, necessary for security, or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

14. Children's Privacy

Smart Village is a general citizen-services application and is not specifically directed toward children.

The application does not knowingly request children to provide unnecessary personal information.

15. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for the information they voluntarily submit to external government websites and services.

Users should verify important service information directly on the relevant official government website before submitting an application or payment.

Never share passwords, OTPs, PINs or other confidential authentication information with another person.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the application, services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

The updated version will be published on this webpage with a revised "Last updated" date.

18. Important Information

Smart Village is an independent application intended to make publicly available information and services easier to discover.

Government websites, service requirements, fees, procedures, policies and availability may change from time to time.

Always verify the latest information on the relevant official government website before proceeding with an important service or application.

19. Free Business Listings and Promotions

Smart Village may display manually added business listings, photographs, banners and promotional information to help villagers discover local businesses and services.

At present, these business listings and promotions are provided free of charge. Smart Village does not collect advertising fees for these listings. These manually added listings are not served through Google AdMob or another third-party advertising-network SDK.

A listing may display business information such as a business name, category, description, contact number, WhatsApp details, address, image or location details. This information may be visible to app users so they can contact or locate the business.

The presence of a business listing does not necessarily mean that Smart Village verifies, recommends or guarantees the business, its products or its services.

If a business owner believes that a listing contains inaccurate information or should be corrected or removed, they may contact the privacy contact listed in this policy.

20. Live News and External News Sources

Smart Village may display news headlines, summaries, images or links obtained from or linking to external news websites or news providers.

When a user opens an external news website, that website may process information in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms. Smart Village does not control the privacy practices of external news websites.

News content is provided for general informational purposes. Users should consult the original publisher for the complete article, updates and corrections.

21. Agricultural Market-Price Information

Smart Village displays agricultural market-price information sourced from AGMARKNET through the Government of India's Open Government Data Platform at data.gov.in.

The market-price synchronization process retrieves the available data from the data.gov.in API and stores market-price records in Firebase Cloud Firestore. The Smart Village application reads the stored records from Firestore to display them to users.

Market prices, arrival quantities, reporting dates and data availability may vary. The information is provided for general reference and should be verified with the relevant market or official source before making a business or trading decision.

The data.gov.in platform and the underlying data provider may process API requests in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

22. Local Notifications

Smart Village may schedule local notifications on a user's device to provide reminders or other app-related information.

Local notifications are scheduled by the application and displayed by the device's operating system. Users can manage notification permissions through their device settings.
